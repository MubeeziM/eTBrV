Help and Support
Getting Started
Creating an eTBr Account
  1. Open art.etbr.org in your browser (Chrome or Edge recommended).
  2. Click Create an Account on the login screen and fill in your full name, username, email and password.
  3. Your account must be approved by an administrator before you can log in. Contact your supervisor or the support team if approval is delayed.
Logging In
  1. Enter your username and password, then click Sign In.
  2. If you forget your password, click Forgot password? and follow the reset instructions sent to your registered email.
  3. For security, you will be automatically signed out after a period of inactivity. Save your work regularly.
Installing the App

The eTBr app can be installed on any device as a Progressive Web App (PWA) — this allows it to work offline and open like a native app:

  • Chrome / Edge on desktop: Click the install icon in the address bar, or click the Install App button at the top of the page.
  • Chrome on Android: Tap the three-dot menu → Add to Home Screen.
  • Safari on iPhone / iPad: Tap the share icon → Add to Home Screen.

Working Offline

The eTBr app is designed to work even when there is no internet connection. Here is how offline mode works:

  • All patient data is stored locally on your device in an encrypted database. You can enter, edit, and view records without any network connection.
  • New and edited records are marked as Pending Sync while offline. The count is shown on the dashboard.
  • When you reconnect to the internet, tap the Sync button to upload your changes and download updates from the server.
  • You can set up an Offline PIN from your profile (My Account → Offline PIN tab) so you can unlock the app without an internet connection.
  • The connection status badge in the header shows Online (green) or Offline (red) at all times.
Data Entry

Click Data Entry on the dashboard to open the registers.

  1. Select a Health Facility from the left-hand panel. If your account is linked to a single facility it will be pre-selected.
  2. Choose a register from the drop-down: ART Register, Unit TB Register, or Presumptive TB Cases.
  3. Fill in the patient form and click Save Patient. The record is saved immediately to your local device.
  4. Saved patients appear in the list below the form. Click any row to view the full record, or click the Edit icon to correct it.
  5. To delete a record, open the patient row and click Delete. Deleted records are retained in the audit log.

ART follow-up visits are entered from the patient list — click the clock icon on an ART patient row to open the visit log.

Monitoring TB Patients

Click Monitor TB Patients on Treatment on the dashboard. The monitoring list highlights:

  • Patients with overdue or upcoming sputum smear tests (months 2, 3, 5, 6).
  • TB patients who are HIV-positive but have not been started on ART or CPT.
  • Patients whose treatment outcome has not been recorded but is due.

Use the filter buttons at the top to focus on a specific monitoring category. You can export the list to Excel to facilitate patient follow-up during the review meetings.

TB/HIV Reports

Click TB/HIV Reports on the dashboard to generate program reports.

  • Select the report type (e.g. DS-TB NTP Quarterly Report, ART Monthly Report), the facility, and the reporting period.
  • Click Generate to produce the report as an Excel file (.xlsx) which you can save, print, or email.
  • For DHIS2 submission, select Generate & Send to DHIS2 — eTBr will map the data to the correct DHIS2 data elements and submit automatically. A confirmation with the DHIS2 import summary will be shown.
Searching for Patients

Click Search Patients on the dashboard, or click the search icon in the statistics bar. You can search across all registers by:

  • Patient name (full or partial)
  • ART number or Unit TB number
  • Phone number
  • Facility name

Search results show which register a patient is in and their current status. Click a result to jump directly to that patient's record.

Data Quality Checks

Click Check Data Quality on the dashboard to run automated quality checks on your DS-TB register. The tool flags:

  • Duplicate Unit TB numbers within the same facility and year.
  • Patients with missing mandatory fields (e.g. no diagnosis method recorded).
  • Inconsistent treatment outcomes (e.g. outcome recorded before treatment started).
  • HIV-positive TB patients who are not on ART or CPT.

Click any issue row to go directly to that patient's record and correct the problem. Export the full quality report to Excel using the Export button.

My Account
  • Edit Profile: Update your display name, email address and profile picture from My Account → Profile.
  • Change Password: Go to My Account → Security to set a new password. Passwords must be at least 8 characters.
  • Offline PIN: Set a 4–6 digit PIN under My Account → Security so you can log in without an internet connection. Keep your PIN confidential.
  • Preferences: Adjust clinical thresholds (e.g. TB lookback window, outcome eligibility window) under My Account → Preferences.

Section A — Patient Registration

ART NumberYesUse the facility's official ART numbering format (e.g. ART/001/2026). Must be unique within the facility. Do not reuse numbers from discharged patients.
Was HIV retested before ART initiation?NoSelect Yes if a confirmatory HIV test was done before starting the patient on ART. Select No if ART was initiated on the basis of the original test only.
Full Patient NameYesEnter the patient's legal full name as written on their identification document. Use mixed case (e.g. Kenyi Abraham), not all caps.
ART Start DateYesThe date the patient took their first dose of ART. This drives all visit-month calculations in the ART register.
Date Enrolled in CareNoThe date the patient was first enrolled in the HIV care programme — this may be earlier than the ART start date if the patient was on pre-ART follow-up.
Date of BirthNoEnter when available. The system will auto-calculate age in years (and lock the age field). Clear the DOB to enter age manually.
Age (years)YesAge at the time of ART initiation, in whole years. For infants under 1 year, enter 0 here and use the Age in months field.
Age in monthsNoOnly for children under 2 years when no date of birth is available. Enter the age in complete months (e.g. 14 months).
SexYesSelect Male or Female. This determines whether the PMTCT section (Section C) is shown.
Contact Phone #1 / #2NoEnter 10-digit phone numbers (starting with 0). Used for patient tracing. Separate multiple contacts by using both fields.
Residence AddressNoNeighbourhood, quarter, or landmark — enough to locate the patient if needed for defaulter tracing.
OccupationNoSelect from the list. If not listed, select Other and type in the field that appears.
Key PopulationNoSelect the most relevant key population group if applicable. Select Other and specify if none of the options match.
Transferred InNoSelect Yes if the patient transferred from another ART site. A field will appear for the name of the referring facility.
Guardian / Parent Name & PhoneNoShown automatically when the patient's age is under 18. Record the name and contact of the parent or legal guardian.

Weight (kg)NoBody weight at ART initiation in kilograms. Used to auto-calculate BMI when Height is also entered.
Height (cm)NoPatient height in centimetres. Together with Weight, auto-calculates BMI.
MUAC (cm)NoMid-Upper Arm Circumference in centimetres. Used for nutritional assessment, particularly in children.
BMI—Calculated automatically from Weight and Height. Read-only.
WHO Clinical StageNoSelect the WHO HIV clinical stage (I–IV) assessed at the time of ART initiation.
CD4 Count / CD4%NoEnter the most recent CD4 result before ART initiation. Tick CD4 is % for children under 5 years whose CD4 is reported as a percentage rather than an absolute count.
CPT Start (Month/Year)NoThe month and year the patient was started on Co-trimoxazole Preventive Therapy (CPT). Use the month picker.
CPT DrugNoSelect the CPT drug prescribed (e.g. Co-trimoxazole 960 mg).
TB Rx Start (Month/Year)NoThe month and year the patient began TB treatment, if they were on TB therapy at ART initiation.
Unit TB Reg NumberNoIf the ART patient is also registered in the TB register, enter the Unit TB number here to link the two records.
TB Status (Baseline)NoSelect the patient's TB status at the time of ART initiation.

Section C — PMTCT (females only)

FieldHow to fill
Breastfeeding StatusSelect whether the patient is currently breastfeeding, was breastfeeding at ART initiation, or is not applicable.
Pregnancies while on ARTClick + Add Pregnancy for each pregnancy that occurred while the patient was on ART. Record the outcome (delivered, miscarriage, etc.) and the date.

Section D — Regimen

Record all regimen changes in chronological order:

  • 1st Line — Original Regimen: Select the first ART regimen the patient was ever started on.
  • 1st Line — Substitution: Use this if a drug in the original 1st-line regimen was substituted (e.g. d4T → TDF) without moving to a new line.
  • 2nd Line: Fill in if and when the patient was switched to a 2nd-line regimen.
  • 3rd Line: Fill in if and when the patient was switched to a 3rd-line regimen.

Section E — ART Follow-up Visits

Follow-up visits are added from the patient list, not from the registration form. Find the patient in the list, click the clock icon, and click Add Visit. For each visit, record:

FieldHow to fill
Visit DateThe date the patient attended the clinic for this visit.
Weight (kg)Current body weight at the time of this visit.
AdherenceSelect the adherence level reported or observed (Good / Fair / Poor).
ARV Dispensed (months)Number of months of ARVs dispensed at this visit.
Viral Load ResultEnter the numeric viral load result (copies/mL). Select Undetectable if the result is below the detection limit.
Viral Load DateDate the viral load sample was collected.
TB Screening ResultSelect the outcome of routine TB screening at this visit.
Patient StatusSelect the patient's current treatment status: Active, Transferred Out, Lost to Follow-up, Died, or Stopped Treatment.
Next Appointment DateThe date the patient is scheduled to return.
Unit TB Register — Field Guidance

Section A — Patient Registration

FieldRequiredHow to fill
Date of RegistrationYesThe date the patient was formally registered in the TB unit register. This is usually the same as or close to the treatment start date.
Unit TB NumberYesUse the facility's sequential Unit TB number for the year (e.g. 001/2026). Numbers must be unique per facility per year. Do not skip numbers; do not reuse numbers.
Patient Full NameYesLegal full name as on ID. Use mixed case. Avoid abbreviations.
Date of BirthNoEnter when available. Auto-calculates and locks the age field.
Age (years)YesAge at registration. For infants under 2 years, use the age in months field instead.
SexYesSelect Male or Female.
Referred ByNoSelect the source of referral (e.g. Out-patient Department, Community, Another Facility).
Village / Boma / Payam / CountyNoPatient's home address. Enter as much detail as available. Payam and County are the administrative units used in South Sudan; adapt terminology to your country's geography if different.
Phone NumberNo10-digit phone number for patient tracing and results notification.

Section B — Diagnosis and Treatment

FieldRequiredHow to fill
TB SiteYesSelect Pulmonary (lungs) or Extra-pulmonary (other sites such as lymph node, spine, pleura). For a patient with both, use Pulmonary.
Type of PatientYesSelect from: New (never treated before or treated for less than 1 month), Relapse, Treatment After Failure, Treatment After Lost to Follow-up, Transfer In, or Other Previously Treated. This is critical for cohort analysis.
Date Treatment StartedNoDate the patient took their first dose of TB medication.
TB Treatment RegimenNoSelect the regimen prescribed (e.g. 2HRZE/4HR for new patients).
Transferred In From (HF)NoShown when patient type is Transfer In. Enter the name of the facility the patient transferred from.
Smear Month 0 ResultNoThe baseline sputum smear result taken at the time of diagnosis. Select the result grade (e.g. 1+, 2+, 3+, Scanty, Negative) and enter the lab date and lab number.
GeneXpert ResultNoSelect the GeneXpert MTB/RIF result (e.g. MTB Detected – RIF Sensitive, MTB Not Detected). Enter the test date alongside.
DST ResultNoDrug Sensitivity Testing result — enter as free text if available.
Method Used To Diagnose TBYesSelect the primary diagnostic method (e.g. Bacteriologically Confirmed by Smear, Bacteriologically Confirmed by GeneXpert, Clinically Diagnosed). This is required for NTP reporting.

Section C — Follow-up Sputum Smears

Record the results of follow-up smear microscopy tests at the standard monitoring time-points. Enter the result, date of test, and lab number for each available time-point. If a test was not done, leave the fields blank — do not enter Not Done as a result.

Time-pointApplies to
End of Month 2All new pulmonary TB patients
End of Month 3Patients still smear-positive at month 2; retreatment patients
End of Month 5All new and retreatment patients (critical for detecting treatment failure)
End of Month 6All retreatment patients; new patients still smear-positive at month 5

Section D — TB/HIV Activities

FieldHow to fill
Date of HIV TestDate the TB patient was tested for HIV. All TB patients should be offered HIV testing.
HIV Test ResultSelect Positive, Negative, or Already Known Positive.
Was the patient started on ART?Select Yes if the HIV-positive TB patient was started on ART. Enter the ART Start Date.
Was the patient started on CPT?Select Yes if the HIV-positive TB patient was started on Co-trimoxazole Preventive Therapy. Enter the CPT Start Date.

Important: All HIV-positive TB patients should be on both ART and CPT. The Monitor TB Patients screen flags patients missing these.

Section E — Treatment Outcome

FieldHow to fill
Treatment OutcomeSelect the final outcome: Cured, Treatment Completed, Died, Treatment Failed, Lost to Follow-up, or Not Evaluated. Record this only when the patient's treatment episode is fully concluded.
Treatment Outcome DateThe date the outcome was determined or the patient left the cohort.
Moved to 2nd line regimen?Select Yes if the patient was switched to a second-line regimen due to treatment failure or drug resistance.
Transferred Out To (HF)Shown when outcome is Transfer Out. Enter the receiving facility name and county.
RemarksUse this free-text field for any clinical notes that do not fit the structured fields — for example, a reason for loss to follow-up or a note about concurrent illness.
General Data Entry Tips
  • Required fields are marked with a red asterisk (*). You cannot save a record with required fields missing.
  • Dates: Always use the date picker — do not type dates manually to avoid format errors.
  • Duplicates: Before adding a new patient, use Search Patients to check they are not already registered. Duplicate records create errors in reports and monitoring.
  • Editing: Click the edit icon on any saved patient to correct a record. All changes are tracked in the audit log.
  • Sync regularly: Sync your data at least once per day when internet is available to ensure the central server stays up to date.
